# Support FAQ

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Go to Settings > Security > Change Password.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval.

Q: What do I do if the API is down or throwing 500 errors?
A: Check our status page at status.example.com. For critical production outages, email emergency@example.com.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: You can cancel anytime from the Billing tab in your user dashboard.
